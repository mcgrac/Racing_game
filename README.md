# {Pokémon Racing Indigo League}

## Racing game featuring 12 Pokémon racers that compete  against an AI and themsleves, to achieve new time records. Different characters have several different stats and a UNIQUE ABILITY! It features a (work in progress) achievement system that works with badges to earn (there are 8 of them).

## Key Features

* 12 different characters with their own stats and abilities. Custom HANDMADE animations and fx for UI and characters.
* AI racer.
* A cool very detailed HANDMADE map with obstacles, different types of ground, and different layers that créate a sense of 3D.
* Game Over screens that showcase the score.
* A really cool start screen with animated buttons and sound effects.
* Achievement and High Score mechanics with their own menus to make you feel Good about spending time in our game.
* Freaking Cleffa is in the game (he's cool like that).

## Controls

Menus:

Arrows for selecting

ENTER: Select

In Game:

* W or UP arrow: accelerate
* A or LEFT arrow: turn left
* D or RIGHT arrow: turn right
* SPACEBAR: special ability
* F: turbo (work in progress)
